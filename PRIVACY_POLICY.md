# Privacy Policy — Drishti Institute

**Last updated:** August 8, 2026

Drishti Institute ("we", "our", "us") operates the Drishti mobile application (the "App"). This page explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding that information.

## Information We Collect

**Account information.** When you sign up, we collect your name, email address, and phone number (if provided), via Firebase Authentication or Google Sign-In.

**Usage data.** We collect information about the courses you view, enroll in, and your progress within the App, stored via Firebase Firestore.

**Device identifiers.** We collect a device identifier (Android ID) for account security and to detect unauthorized multi-device logins.

**Payment information.** When you purchase a course, payment is processed by Razorpay. We do not store your card, UPI, or bank details — these are handled directly by Razorpay under its own privacy policy and PCI-DSS compliance. We receive only confirmation of payment success/failure and a transaction reference.

**Push notification tokens.** We collect a Firebase Cloud Messaging token to send you notifications about your courses and account.

## How We Use Information

- To create and manage your account
- To provide access to purchased courses and track your learning progress
- To process payments and prevent fraud
- To send you service-related notifications
- To respond to support requests sent to our contact email

## Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share data only with:
- **Firebase (Google)** — for authentication, database, and notifications
- **Razorpay** — for payment processing

Both providers are contractually bound to protect your data under their own privacy policies.

## Data Retention

We retain your account and usage data as long as your account is active. You may request deletion of your account and associated data by contacting us at the email below.

## Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13. Users aged 13-18 should use the App under parental/guardian consent. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

## Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data at any time by contacting us.

## Security

We use industry-standard measures (Firebase Authentication, encrypted network connections) to protect your data. No system is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
